This policy explains what ClearPath Automotive collects through this pre-opening website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Effective September 20, 2026

Information we collect

When you join the founding list, we collect your email address and ZIP code. We also record the signup time, the page or form used, your consent to receive opening updates, and email delivery information. If you contact us or use the ClearPath advisor, we receive the information you choose to include in that conversation. Our hosting providers may collect ordinary technical data such as IP address, browser type, and request logs for security and reliability.

We do not currently ask founding-list members for a street address, phone number, payment information, or vehicle history.

How we use it

  • To confirm your founding-list signup and send meaningful opening milestones.
  • To understand broad regional interest through ZIP-code totals.
  • To answer messages, operate the website, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.
  • To notify founding-list members when scheduling becomes available.

We do not sell personal information or manufacture urgency around our opening.

Services that help us operate

We use Netlify to host the site and receive forms, Supabase to store founding-list records, Resend to deliver email, and Anthropic to support the optional website advisor. These providers process information on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect people, the business, or our systems.

Email choices and retention

You may unsubscribe through the link in an update email or by writing to info@driveclearpath.com. We keep founding-list information while you remain subscribed and while it is useful for the stated pre-opening purpose. We may retain limited records when needed for security, legal compliance, or to honor an unsubscribe request.

Security and your choices

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. You may ask to access, correct, or delete the information you submitted by contacting us from the same email address. We will verify and respond to reasonable requests.

Children and policy updates

This site and founding list are not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy as ClearPath develops; the effective date above will show the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated where appropriate.
