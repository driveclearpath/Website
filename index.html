Why ClearPath

A repair shop designed to lower the temperature.

The hardest part of auto repair is often not the repair. It is the uncertainty around it.

We are designing ClearPath around a simple idea: when technical truth is documented and communication is clear, customers can make good decisions without pressure. That means a calm welcome, a real inspection, a written plan, and updates before you have to ask.

01

See what we see.

Every vehicle starts with a documented inspection. Photos and video turn technical findings into something you can actually understand.

02

No manufactured urgency.

We separate what needs attention now from what should be planned next. You get priorities—not a wall of red designed to frighten you.

03

Capacity is protected.

We schedule around the work our team can complete well. A full parking lot is not success if communication and craftsmanship suffer.

04

Quality has a system.

Repairs are verified, vehicles are checked before delivery, and problems trigger process improvement—not finger-pointing.

What to expect

A clear path from concern to confidence.

No mystery handoffs. No surprise change in scope. Each step creates the clarity needed for the next one.

  1. 1

    We listen first.

    Your concern is documented in your words, along with the context that helps us investigate it properly.

  2. 2

    We establish the truth.

    A technician inspects and documents the vehicle before recommendations, pricing, or promises are made.

  3. 3

    We build the plan together.

    We explain findings, priorities, options, and timing. You authorize the work you choose—in writing.

  4. 4

    We keep you ahead of the day.

    If timing or scope changes, you hear from us before you have to wonder what is happening.

  5. 5

    We verify the result.

    The repair and vehicle are checked before delivery, then explained clearly when the keys return to you.

A note from the founder

Built from experience on the shop floor.

I have spent years inside an independent repair shop, most recently responsible for its day-to-day operations. I have seen what happens when talented people are forced to rely on memory, urgency, and heroics—and how quickly trust suffers when the process is unclear.

ClearPath Automotive is the shop I believe customers and good technicians deserve: disciplined without being cold, transparent without being theatrical, and organized enough to keep its promises.

We have written those standards down before opening the doors. They govern how we inspect, communicate, schedule, authorize, repair, verify, and improve. The goal is not to be the busiest shop. It is to become the shop you trust for the life of your vehicle.

BF
Brad FournierFounder · ClearPath Automotive
